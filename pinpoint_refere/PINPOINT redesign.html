What changed

Every place carries its jeep. Route plates, like the painted signboards behind a jeepney windshield, show which jeep, the fare and where to say “para”. That is the job of the app, so it became the look of the app.

Home asks one question. Three equal tiles became one main action and two supporting ones.

The map labels only the pin you tap. The old one stacked four labels on top of each other. The “API key required” watermark in your screenshots comes from the CARTO tile provider settings, not the UI.

Login button is solid. The current one reads as disabled. Profile is grouped by what people do, admin tools sit apart, and Delete account asks first.
